Privacy Policy

App Name: Gujarati Kakko Fun
Developer: ajenixlab
Last updated: October 3, 2026

Gujarati Kakko Fun ("we", "our", or "us") is an educational application designed to help children learn Gujarati letters and basic Gujarati language concepts through interactive learning activities.

We respect the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use Gujarati Kakko Fun and how third-party services used by the application may handle information.

1. Information We Collect

Gujarati Kakko Fun does not require users to create an account, provide their name, email address, phone number, or other personal information in order to use the core learning features of the app.

We do not operate our own user account system or require users to submit personal information directly to us.

Some learning progress, preferences, and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device so that the application can provide its learning features. This local information is not intended to identify the user and is not uploaded to our servers by the core learning functionality.

2. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

Gujarati Kakko Fun may display advertisements using Google's Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

Because the application is intended for children, advertising is configured and served in accordance with applicable Google Play Families requirements and Google's child-directed advertising requirements.

Google Mobile Ads may process certain information automatically, depending on the SDK configuration and features being used. This may include information such as:

  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information.
  • IP address and related technical information.
  • Certain device or application identifiers.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention purposes and is encrypted in transit.

For information about Google's data practices, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Mobile Ads Data Disclosure

3. Children's Privacy

Gujarati Kakko Fun is designed for children and families and is intended to provide age-appropriate educational content.

We do not knowingly require children to provide personal information such as their name, email address, telephone number, or physical address in order to use the application's core learning features.

Our use of third-party services, including advertising services, is subject to the applicable policies and requirements for child-directed applications.

Important: Gujarati Kakko Fun does not provide a social networking, public messaging, or user-to-user communication feature.

4. Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services to provide advertising and related technical functionality.

The primary third-party service currently used for advertising is:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) - Google Privacy Policy

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage parents and guardians to review the privacy practices of these services.

5. Data Security

Information transmitted by third-party services used by the application may be protected using encryption in transit.

We do not operate a user account database for Gujarati Kakko Fun's core learning functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Gujarati Kakko Fun does not provide user accounts that can be created or managed within the application.

Local application data, such as locally stored preferences or learning progress, can be removed by uninstalling the application or clearing the application's data through the device's operating system.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the respective third party's retention and deletion practices.

7. Permissions

Gujarati Kakko Fun does not require access to the following personal device features for its core learning functionality:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • Phone calls

The application may use standard Android system capabilities required to display content, save local application data, and provide advertising functionality.

8. In-App Purchases

If premium features or purchases are made available in Gujarati Kakko Fun, payment processing is handled through the applicable app store payment system.

We do not receive or store users' full credit card, debit card, or bank account information.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the application, third-party services, legal requirements, or our data practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy and publish the updated policy at this URL.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Gujarati Kakko Fun, please contact us.
